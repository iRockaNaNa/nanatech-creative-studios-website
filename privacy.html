Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Plain English first: we collect as little as possible. We don't sell your information, we don't run ads that track you, and we don't use analytics on this website. This page is a plain-English summary of how Nanatech Creative Studios handles information. It is a general draft, not legal advice.

The short version

If you just browse this website, we collect nothing about you directly. If you sign up for our monthly email, you give us your name and email address so we can send it. If you order a 3D print, you give us the details we need to make it and ship it. Our apps keep your stuff on your own device. We never sell your personal information to anyone.

1. This website

What we collect: nothing directly. There is no account to create, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no advertising on this site. We don't set cookies and we don't store anything in your browser.

What others may see: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and it loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, that means GitHub and Google can see the standard technical details of your visit (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the page. We don't receive or use that information.

Links to other sites: this site links out to Twitch, YouTube, X, Discord, GitHub, and Google Drive. If you click one of those links, you leave our site and that service's own privacy policy applies.

2. Monthly email list

Our email signup is a Google Form. When you sign up, you give us:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your email address (required, so we can send the email)
  • What you want updates about — streams and schedule, 3D printing, apps, or everything

We use that information only to send you the monthly email — stream news, the 3D printing launch, and app updates, once a month, no spam. Your answers are stored in our Google Forms account. We don't share the list with anyone, and we don't sell it. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can email us anytime and ask to be removed.

3. 3D printing orders

Our print shop is still in setup, so orders aren't open yet. When the shop opens and you order a custom print, we'll ask for what we need to make and ship it:

  • Your name and email address — so we can talk to you about your order
  • Your shipping address — so we can send your print to you
  • Your order details — what you want printed and how you want it

Payment is handled by a third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number. We keep your order information only as long as we need it to finish your order and handle any follow-up, and we don't share it except as needed to ship your order (for example, with the shipping carrier).

4. TypeReady (typing test app)

TypeReady needs no account, shows no ads, and works offline. Your best test scores are saved only on your own device. Nothing you type and no score ever leaves your device or gets sent to us.

5. Chance of Bananas (weather app)

Chance of Bananas needs no account and shows no ads. It uses your location only if you allow it, and only to get your forecast:

  • Your location (when you use GPS) is sent to Open-Meteo, the National Weather Service, and BigDataCloud — only to fetch your forecast and turn your coordinates into a city name. We don't store your location anywhere.
  • Your saved cities and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • Premium purchases are handled by Google Play. We don't see or store your payment details.
  • Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, the National Weather Service, and RainViewer (radar), and map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. Those services see the standard technical details of the request, the same as any app that loads data from the internet.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We don't share your information except as described on this page (for example, to ship an order or send the email you signed up for).
  • We don't run analytics on this website and we don't use tracking cookies.
  • We don't show ads on this website or in our apps.

7. Children's privacy

Our website, email list, and apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

You are in control of your information:

  • Unsubscribe from the monthly email at any time using the link in the email, or by emailing us.
  • Ask us what personal information we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ciao@nanatechcreativestudios.com.
  • Weather app: you can turn off location access for Chance of Bananas in your device settings at any time — the app still works if you search for a city by name instead.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. Big changes — like opening the print shop or adding a new app — will get a note here.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at ciao@nanatechcreativestudios.com or call/text our business line at (623) 628-6342.
